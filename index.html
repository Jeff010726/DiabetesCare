FAQ

Frequently Asked Question

What is DSMES?

Diabetes Self-Management Education and Support helps patients understand monitoring, medication routines, meals, movement, and problem solving.

Do I need a provider order?

A provider order is usually needed for insurance-based DSMES or MNT services.

What equipment do I need?

Bring your glucose meter, CGM app, pump supplies, medication list, and recent care plan when available.

Where are sessions held?

Programs may be online or in person depending on class format, language cohort, and scheduling.
