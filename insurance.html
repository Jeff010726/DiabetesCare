Insurance-first onboarding

Many patients can use health insurance for diabetes education.

Coverage commonly requires a signed order from the clinician managing the patient's diabetes care. We help verify benefits before class placement.

Branded referral order Original order PDF Ask us to verify
Covered service types

DSMES/T and MNT are ordered services, not a retail class purchase.

The referral order can request initial DSMES/T, follow-up DSMES/T, initial MNT, annual MNT follow-up, specific content areas, and device training.

Initial DSMES/T can be ordered up to 10 hours when clinically appropriate.

Follow-up DSMES/T can be ordered for ongoing support.

Initial MNT and annual follow-up MNT may be covered for eligible patients.

Coverage varies by plan, payer rules, diagnosis, referral completeness, and benefits.

What patients need

The coverage hook works best when the process is clear.

03Scheduling

Important coverage note

Insurance coverage is not guaranteed. XT Diabetes Care can help verify benefits and submit eligible services, but final coverage and patient responsibility are determined by the health plan.
