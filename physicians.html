Clinical fit

Ideal referrals are patients who need structured self-management education.

Newly diagnosed patients, patients starting injectables or devices, patients with confusing glucose patterns, and patients who have not received formal DSMES can benefit from a dedicated education pathway outside the short office visit.

Your benefit

We function like an education extension of the clinic.

Better prepared patients

Patients learn how to monitor, eat, move, inject, troubleshoot, and bring useful data back to follow-up visits.

Less repetition in visits

Education topics that consume appointment time are handled systematically in class.

Clear boundaries

We reinforce your care plan and remind patients to follow up. We do not replace prescribing or medical decision-making.

Referral workflow

A simple order-based process.

01Complete order

Select DSMES/T and/or MNT, diagnosis, content areas, provider signature, and NPI.

02Send documents

Include recent labs when available for eligibility and outcomes monitoring.

03Patient education

We schedule class, document attendance, and reinforce follow-up with the treating clinician.
